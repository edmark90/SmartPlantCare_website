FAQ

Common Questions

You can use the app for plant identification and disease scanning without connecting a sensor. Start by adding a supported plant and taking a clear photo of its leaves. For soil moisture readings and other sensor-based measurements, you need a compatible sensor module paired with the system. Automatic irrigation also requires the connected watering hardware; the Android app alone cannot water your plants. Follow the setup instructions provided with your hardware before enabling automatic care.

AI disease detection provides an initial assessment of visible symptoms in a leaf photo. Results depend on image quality, lighting, the plant species, and whether the condition is represented in the model. For a clearer scan, photograph a single affected leaf in natural light, keep it in focus, and avoid strong shadows or a busy background. A healthy result does not guarantee that a plant is free of every disease. Compare the result with the plant's actual condition and ask an agricultural specialist if symptoms persist or spread.

The current build focuses on three commonly grown vegetables: tomato, eggplant, and chili. These plants are the starting point for the available care profiles and supported scanning experience. Choose the correct plant profile when adding a plant so the information stays relevant to what you are growing. Identification and disease results for other species may be unavailable or unreliable. Support for additional plants is planned, but availability will depend on future development and validation.

Plant profiles and sensor readings are associated with the account used in the app. Use a strong, unique password, keep your login details private, and sign out when using a shared device. Before uploading photos, check that the image does not include personal documents, people, or location details you do not intend to share. This capstone website does not yet provide a published privacy policy describing storage, retention, or deletion procedures. Contact the development team through the linked project channels for clarification before submitting sensitive information.
