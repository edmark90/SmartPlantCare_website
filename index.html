One pipeline, three jobs

The same loop runs quietly in the background of every plant you register — sense, analyze, act.

01 / SENSE

Sensors capture conditions

Soil moisture, light, and temperature are logged every few minutes and streamed to your phone in real time.

02 / ANALYZE

AI checks the readings

Leaf photos and sensor data are compared against known healthy and diseased patterns to flag issues early.

03 / ACT

The system responds

Watering, alerts, and the care calendar adjust automatically — no manual timers or guesswork required.
