Common Questions

The app works on its own for plant identification and disease scanning. Real-time soil, light, and temperature monitoring requires a paired sensor module.

The model is trained to flag common disease and pest patterns early with a confidence score, and is intended as a first read — not a replacement for expert diagnosis.

The current build ships with care profiles for tomato, eggplant, and chili, with more species planned.

Plant and sensor data is tied to your account and is not shared with third parties.
